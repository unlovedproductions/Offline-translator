## Problem

A guest who does not share your language is at the desk. You have one tablet, a noisy room, and Wi‑Fi that drops when the lobby fills up.

Consumer translator apps assume each person has a phone, a data plan, and patience for cloud round-trips. That fails at a shared counter: no operational record, no push-to-talk for the lobby, and no workflow when the guest network is down.

**Why this section:** Names the job (shared counter) so Google Translate is the wrong comparison, not the enemy.

---

## Solution

**Puente** is a shared-device conversational interpreter for Android.

Two people take turns on one tablet. Puente recognizes speech on-device, translates it, speaks the result aloud, and keeps a transcript you can export — so the exchange survives dead zones after setup, and the next shift can read what was asked.

---

## Benefits

- **One tablet, two speakers** — Speaker A/B and language swap so staff and guest are not juggling two phones.
- **Offline after setup** — Download translation packs once over Wi‑Fi; recognition and translation then run without a network.
- **A log you can hand off** — Search, favorite, and export TXT / PDF / CSV when the visit ends.
- **Built for a real lobby** — Optional push-to-talk, silence auto-stop, mic level, and confidence on each turn.

---

## Feature blocks

### Conversation modes
Auto English ↔ Spanish with language detection, plus manual English ↔ Spanish, English ↔ French, and English ↔ German. Set a start language and swap sides when roles flip.

### Clear turns under pressure
Push-to-talk, silence auto-stop, mic level feedback, and optional noise reduction keep turns usable in a real room.

### Confidence you can act on
See recognition confidence on each turn. Retry unclear speech or translate anyway when the line behind them is growing.

### Transcript and favorites
Open **Transcript** for searchable history with timestamps. Star the address, the allergy, the room number.

### Exportable bilingual logs
Export to TXT, PDF, or CSV and share through the system share sheet. Export history stays on the device until you share it.

### Offline phrasebook
Common English/Spanish phrases when you do not need a full conversation.

## How it works

1. **Buy once** — $29 on Gumroad. Sideload the signed APK (Android 6.0+).

## Trust and privacy

- Speech recognition uses on-device Vosk models (English, Spanish, French, German bundled with the app).
- Translation uses Google ML Kit on-device models after a Wi‑Fi download. Google may collect ML Kit utilization metrics; this is not “nothing ever leaves the device.”
- Spoken playback uses on-device TTS voices only.
- Core conversation flow does not use cloud STT or cloud translation APIs once models are cached.
- Exports leave the device only when a user explicitly shares them.
- Android backup of app data is disabled.
- License is **Apache-2.0**: you are paying for the signed build and support, not exclusive rights.
