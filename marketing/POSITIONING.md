# Puente — Positioning

Product marketing foundation. April Dunford method. No paying customers yet — ICP and “when we win” are 🔴 assumed until first sales and win/loss exist.

**Do not claim:** HIPAA, air-gap, Play Store, iOS, user counts, accuracy %, or reviews.

---

## Bottom line

Puente is a **shared-device conversational interpreter** for Android, not a tourist translator.

**Primary buyer:** a hospitality, retail, or field supervisor who puts **one Android tablet on the counter** and needs English ↔ Spanish (or FR/DE) when Wi‑Fi is weak and no bilingual staffer is on that shift.

**Do not lead with clinics.** There is no BAA, no HIPAA posture, and ML Kit may send utilization metrics. Clinic copy is a liability, not a TAM.

**Category:** shared-device conversational interpreter (Android APK).  
**Not:** “offline translator app,” “AI interpreter platform,” or “medical translation.”

---

## Positioning statement

FOR front-of-house and field teams who share one Android tablet  
WHO need to finish a bilingual conversation when connectivity is poor and no interpreter is on shift  
PUENTE IS A shared-device conversational interpreter  
THAT lets two people take turns speaking, hear the translation aloud, and keep an exportable transcript — on one device, offline after setup  
UNLIKE Google Translate (personal phone, cloud-first conversation, no operational log) and Pocketalk / Timekettle (dedicated hardware at ~$280–$350)  
OUR PRODUCT is built for turn-taking on a shared counter device: speaker A/B, push-to-talk, confidence on each turn, and TXT/PDF/CSV export.

**Value proposition:** Puente helps counter and field teams complete English/Spanish (or French/German) conversations on one Android tablet by running speech, translation, and playback on-device after setup.

## ICP (🔴 assumed — no customer data)

### Primary: shared-counter operator

| Dimension | Target | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Role | Owner, GM, or front-desk lead | Buys the tablet workflow; uses it or trains staff |
| Setting | Hotel desk, café, shop, warehouse gate, community office | One device, two speakers, noisy room |
| Language | English ↔ Spanish first; FR/DE as extras | Auto mode is EN↔ES only |
| Constraint | Intermittent Wi‑Fi, sideload allowed or IT-managed | Gumroad APK, not Play |
| Budget | Will spend tens of dollars, not $350 hardware or a staff interpreter | Software on hardware they already own |
| Risk tolerance | Accepts “offline after first download,” not air-gap | Honest ML Kit terms |

**Economic buyer:** owner / GM.  
**User:** whoever is on the counter.  
**Technical buyer:** none, or a one-person IT shop who can sideload.

### Secondary (do not market first)

- Bilingual households who already share one phone and will sideload.
- Community orgs that want a transcript, not a tourist camera translate.

### Explicit non-ICP

- Tourists comparing to free Google Translate on a personal phone.
- Clinics, hospitals, legal intake (compliance theater).
- Teams that need 20+ languages or simultaneous (not turn-taking) interpretation.
- iOS-only shops.
- Buyers who refuse unknown-source APKs and will wait for Play.

### ICP validation (not met)

- [ ] 5+ paying customers match this profile
- [ ] Fastest sales cycles
- [ ] Highest LTV
- [ ] Lowest refund rate
- [ ] Willing to be named

Until then, treat hospitality/front-of-house as a **hypothesis**, not a segment you have earned.

| Tier | Who | What buyers actually do |
|------|-----|-------------------------|
| 1 Direct | Google Translate conversation mode | Free, 100+ languages, conversation + offline packs. Personal-phone UX. Weak operational record. |
| 1 Direct | Microsoft Translator | Free, multi-device conversation. Needs each person on their own phone. |

| 2 Adjacent | Pocketalk S2-class handhelds | Dedicated device, ~$350, many languages, cellular plans. |
| 2 Adjacent | Timekettle WT2 / W4 earbuds | ~$280–$349, simultaneous two-way, extra hardware. |
| 3 Status quo | Bilingual staffer, typed notes, speakerphone to a friend, on-call interpreter | Interpreter callouts often $50–$150/hour with a minimum. |

### Unique attributes (only what the product actually has)

1. **One device, two speakers** — speaker A/B, start language + swap, turn-taking loop (STT → translate → TTS).
2. **Offline after first translation-pack download** — bundled Vosk speech models; ML Kit packs over Wi‑Fi once.
3. **Operational transcript** — search, favorites, confidence, export TXT/PDF/CSV.
4. **Counter UX** — optional PTT, silence auto-stop, mic level, noise reduction.
5. **Narrow language set done as a conversation** — Auto EN↔ES; manual EN↔ES / EN↔FR / EN↔DE.

### Attribute → value

| Attribute | Why the ICP cares |
|-----------|-------------------|
| One shared tablet | Guest does not unlock their phone; staff does not juggle two apps |
| Offline after setup | Basement desk, rural gate, “guest Wi‑Fi is down” |
| Transcript + export | Handoff to the next shift; keep the address or request |
| PTT + confidence | Noisy lobby; retry the turn instead of guessing |
| Four languages | Matches EN/ES-heavy US/EU counters; not a 100-language claim |

### When we win / lose

**Win:** buyer already has an Android tablet on the counter, lives in EN↔ES (or FR/DE), hates passing two phones, needs a log, will sideload.

**Lose:** buyer wants 40 languages, simultaneous earbuds, Play Store only, iPhone, medical/legal cover, or “just use Google Translate for free.”

## Battlecards (short)

### Google Translate

- **They say:** conversation + offline packs, free, every language.
- **Reality:** excellent personal-phone tool. Not a shared-counter workflow. No staff export trail.
- **Talk track:** “Use Translate on your own phone. Puente is for the tablet that stays on the desk.”

### Pocketalk / Timekettle

- **They say:** dedicated hardware, dozens of languages, simultaneous.
- **Reality:** $280–$350 plus accessories; another device to charge and lose.
- **Talk track:** “If you already own the Android tablet, you are buying the workflow, not another gadget.”

### Human interpreter

- **They say:** accurate, certified, the right call for clinical/legal.
- **Reality:** they are right for those jobs. Puente is not a substitute for certified interpreting.
- **Talk track:** “Puente covers the 2-minute desk exchange so you do not burn a callout on ‘where is the bathroom’ / ‘the room is ready.’”

## Claims discipline

Allowed: on-device Vosk STT; ML Kit translation after Wi‑Fi download; on-device TTS; transcript/export; Android 6.0+.

Forbidden: 100% offline forever; HIPAA; air-gap; “nothing ever leaves the device”; accuracy guarantees; “trusted by N businesses.”
